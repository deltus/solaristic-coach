# Round 2 brief: the roll and the post-session swipe

## Preview link

https://deltus.github.io/solaristic-coach/preview/round2/

(It has its own data, separate from the round 1 preview. Tap **Load demo team** in the pink PREVIEW strip to get the new five-week Riverside Rockets demo.)

## What changed

- **Roll:** everyone starts as "here". Tap a player to get big buttons: Here, Late, Sick, Injured, Away, No-show.
- **Post-session check:** one card per player who came (12 swipes, not 36). Swipe left = all good, up = star of the session, right = something to note.
- **Note chips** (no typing): effort, behaviour, skill, wellbeing and social, including "Conflict with…" where you pick the other player.
- **Voice note:** optional, per player, up to 30 seconds, kept on the phone only.
- **Game cards** show each player's minutes and positions from the game clock.
- **Log now:** a button on the session and live game screens for something that happens mid-session. The chips show up on that player's card afterwards, and a quick "all good" swipe never wipes them.
- **Safety prompt:** "Seemed upset" or "Knock or injury" shows "Report this to your club's child safety officer", on the card and on the end summary.
- **Training focus:** pick one skill for the session (optional).
- **Sports:** futsal added; every sport has a skill list (5–8 skills); team sizes are now 4–15.
- **Season summary** shows absence reasons and the check counts (stars, notes, which chips). Wellbeing and injury chips are left off the printout.
- **New demo:** the same 12-player U11 soccer squad, now 5 weeks (5 trainings, 4 games), with absence reasons, stars, notes and a few patterns to spot (Jack's rough play, Zara left out, Mia missing sessions, Oliver's knock).
- **Your round 1 data is upgraded, not wiped:** old "Away" becomes "Absent: away", and old ratings and notes are kept and still count.

## How to test (on your phone)

**Roll**
1. Open the preview link. Tap **Load demo team**, then **OK**.
2. Tap **Start training**. Everyone on the roll already says **Here**.
3. Tap a player, then **Sick**. The row closes and says Sick. Tap another, then **Late**. The count at the top updates.
4. Under **Today's focus**, tap a skill (e.g. Passing).

**Log now**
5. Tap the red **＋ Log now** button, pick a player, tap **Rough play**, then **Conflict with…** and pick another player. Tap **Log it**. You're back on the session.

**Post-session check** (the main test; time yourself)
6. Tap **Close session and check players**.
7. For each card, use one thumb: swipe **left** (all good), **up** (star), or tap **★ Star** / **← All good** at the bottom. The sick player has no card.
8. On the player you logged in step 5, the card says "Logged during the session…". Swipe left: it should still be kept as a note (check the summary at the end).
9. On one player, swipe **right** (or tap **Note →**). Tap **Knock or injury**: the safety message appears. Tap **Save note**.
10. Try **🎙 Voice note** on one card: allow the microphone, say something, tap **Stop**, then **▶** to play it back.
11. At the end, check the summary: counts, the list of stars and notes, and the safety message naming the player.
12. How long did 12 cards take? The goal is under 90 seconds for 15.

**Games**
13. Back on the team, open the **v Westgate Wanderers** game, then tap **Check players** at the bottom. Each card shows "NN min · positions".
14. **Start game**, run the clock for a minute, tap **＋ Log now** on the game screen, log something, and check that you go back to the game.

**The rest**
15. **Season summary:** Jack shows "Rough play ×…", and absences show reasons (e.g. "1 sick"). Oliver's knock is *not* printed.
16. **Teams → + Add another team → Futsal:** check the drawing and that the team sizes offered are 4 or 5.
17. **Settings → Back up**, then **Restore**: everything comes back, including a voice note.

## Not done / unsure

- **Voice notes are tested in desktop Chrome only.** iPhone Safari should work (it records in a different format), but please try it. If the microphone prompt never appears, check Settings → Safari → Microphone.
- **Season dots, patterns and profiles are round 3.** The chips are stored with a colour (green / amber / red) ready for them. "Needs attention" still uses round 1's rules (minutes, positions, missed sessions).
- **Game cards show minutes, but you can't edit them yet.** If you didn't run the clock, there's nothing to fix from. A quick "fix minutes" card comes with the match-day planner (round 5).
- **The note sheet needs a short scroll** on a small phone (5 groups, 15 chips). The Save button stays pinned at the bottom.
- **The team builder** now uses stars and drill chips as a rough strength score until round 3 adds proper skill ratings (1–5).
- **AFL** offers 12 or 15 a side (the 4–15 range). A team you'd saved at 18 shows as 15.
- **Cricket and hockey** were kept (not in the new list); tell me if you'd rather hide them.
- **Built on round 1:** round 1 isn't merged yet, so saying "merge" on round 2 puts both live together.
